================================================================================
  1. CHARACTER STATS  (StatKey.java, CharacterStats.java)
================================================================================

The ten raw base stats defined per character. StatKey is the single source of
truth: each constant owns its camelCase JSON field name, display label, and
lookup aliases.

  Range:      MIN_STAT = 10 (minimum) to MAX_STAT = 300 (maximum).
  Baseline:   BASELINE = 80 (default, and the point-buy starting value).
  N/A:        value 0 means the stat is absent. Used for CURSED_TECHNIQUE_MASTERY
              on characters without an innate technique. Editors are unclamped;
              gameplay clamps to [10, 300].

  StatKey constants (label / aliases):

    VITALITY                       vitality / vit
      Determines maximum HP.
      HP = round(S(Vitality) × HP_PER_VIT),  HP_PER_VIT = 3.5  (S = StatScale).
      Baseline 80 → 280 HP. Max 300 → ~1652 HP (S(300)=472 — see STAT SCALING).

    STRENGTH                       strength / str
      Physical striking power. Primary driver of Power for PHYSICAL moves
      (4:1 with Combat Ability).

    DURABILITY                     durability / dur
      Physical resilience. Weighted 2 in the Defense formula (alongside CE
      reinforcement, weighted 6).

    SPEED                          speed / spd
      Movement and reaction speed. Primary driver of AP Bar size and Evasion;
      secondary driver of Accuracy.

    COMBAT_ABILITY                 combatAbility / ca
      General combat aptitude. Primary driver of Accuracy; secondary driver of
      AP Bar size and PHYSICAL Power. Determines the COMBAT_ARTS slot budget.

    CURSED_ENERGY_RESERVES         cursedEnergyReserves / cer
      Total CE pool available in a fight (no mid-fight regen). Weighted 2 in the
      CE Power component and supplies the CE term of Defense.

    CURSED_ENERGY_EFFICIENCY       cursedEnergyEfficiency / ceeff
      Modifies the CE cost of all CE-tagged moves. 80 = neutral cost.
      actualCost = baseCeCost × (80 / efficiency), clamped to [minCeCost, maxCeCost].

    CURSED_ENERGY_OUTPUT           cursedEnergyOutput / ceo
      Raw cursed energy output. Primary driver (3:2:1) of the CE Power component,
      and caps CE reinforcement in the Defense formula (× DEFENSE_CE_CAP_FACTOR).

    JUJUTSU_SKILL                  jujutsuSkill / js
      Governs NON_INNATE_TECHNIQUE moves and barriers / RCT / cursed-tool styles.
      Determines the JUJUTSU_ARTS slot budget. Secondary AP Bar driver.

    CURSED_TECHNIQUE_MASTERY       cursedTechniqueMastery / ctm
      Only meaningful for characters with an innate cursed technique. Scales
      INNATE_TECHNIQUE Power (50:50 with the CE component).
      Does NOT grant move slots — slots come from JUJUTSU_SKILL and COMBAT_ABILITY
      only. Set to 0 (N/A) for characters without an innate technique.

  Lookup: StatKey.fromString normalises to lowercase and strips spaces and
  underscores before matching aliases or fieldName. Throws on no match.

  2. BATTLE STATS  (BattleStatKey.java)
================================================================================

Derived or battle-only values that can be modified at runtime by abilities
(BATTLE_STAT_* effects, statuses). Distinct from base stats (StatKey).

    MAX_HP            Max HP
    MAX_CE            Max Cursed Energy
    MAX_AP            Max AP (next planning budget)
    ACCURACY          Accuracy
    EVASION          Evasion
    POWER             Power (per-move multiplier)
    DEFENSE           Defense (computed value)
    DAMAGE_DEALT      Outgoing damage multiplier
    DAMAGE_TAKEN      Incoming damage multiplier
    CE_COST           CE cost multiplier
    BLACK_FLASH_CHANCE  Black Flash chance
    HEALING           HP healing multiplier
    CE_RESTORATION    CE restoration multiplier

  Lookup: BattleStatKey.fromString uppercases and replaces spaces with '_'.

================================================================================
  3. COMBAT STATS  (CombatStats.java)
================================================================================

Derived values computed from base stats at combatant load time, plus the static
damage-resolution helpers. These are the numbers actually used in combat.

  Tuning constants (lines noted):
    HP_PER_VIT            = 3.5      (L99,  final)
    AP_DIVISOR            = 18       (L108, final)
    HIT_BALANCE_FACTOR    = 1/19     (L115, final)  → equal ACC/EVA = 95% on a 1.0 move
    CE_POOL_SCALE         = 8        (L118, final)
    ART_SLOT_TIERS        = table    (see §7)  → baseline 80 = 6 slots, max 15 at 300
    BF_BASE_CHANCE        = 0.03     (L124, PLACEHOLDER)
    BFS_BF_CHANCES        = {0.10, 0.20, 0.35, 0.50}  (L127, final)
    BF_DAMAGE_MULTIPLIER  = 2.5      (L130, final)
    BF_CE_RESTORE_FRACTION= 0.05     (L133, PLACEHOLDER)
    DEFENSE_CE_CAP_FACTOR = 0.5      (L142, final)
    PHYSICAL_POWER_MULT   = 0.85     (after CombatStats constants; PHYSICAL only)

  STAT SCALING (StatScale.java) — applied to EVERY base stat before any formula:
    S(s) = max(8, round(80 + sign(s − 80) · 0.12 · |s − 80|^1.5)),  anchored at S(80)=80.
    Flattens at the baseline (derivative 0) and steepens away: stat differences matter
    MORE the further from 80 — a 300-vs-250 gap dwarfs a 100-vs-200 gap — while low
    stats stay meaningfully worse without collapsing (S(20)=24, S(40)=50, S(60)=69).
    Applied symmetrically to both sides of every comparison (Power/Defense, ACC/EVA…).
    Sample: s= 10 20 40 60 80 100 120 150 200 250 300
            S= 10 24 50 69 80  91 110 150 238 346 472

  Stored derived values (computed once per combatant; inputs are SCALED via S()):
    Max HP          = round(S(Vitality) × HP_PER_VIT)
    Max AP Bar      = (S(Speed) × 15 + S(CombatAbility) × 3) / AP_DIVISOR  → baseline 80
    Accuracy        = (S(CombatAbility) × 4 + S(Speed)) / 5                 → baseline 80
    Evasion         = (S(Speed) × 4 + S(CombatAbility)) / 5                → baseline 80
    Max CE (pool)   = round(S(CursedEnergyReserves) × CE_POOL_SCALE)       → baseline 80 = 640
    Combat Arts slots = artSlotsFor(CombatAbility)   ← RAW stat, threshold table (§7) → baseline 6
    Jujutsu Arts slots= artSlotsFor(JujutsuSkill)    ← RAW stat, threshold table (§7) → baseline 6

  DEFENSE — computed per hit, NOT stored.
    The defender's resilience at the moment of each hit. CE reinforcement is
    CAPPED by Cursed Energy Output, NOT by pool size. While the pool can supply
    at least the cap, Defense holds at a plateau — spending CE early in a round
    costs no Defense. Once the pool drops below the cap, Defense degrades
    (steeply, since the CE term is weighted 6). Lore-accurate: a bottomless-CE
    character (e.g. Yuta, Hakari) still has finite Defense because their OUTPUT,
    not their pool, limits reinforcement.

      ceFromPool       = S(CursedEnergyReserves) × (currentCe / maxCe)
      reinforcementCap = S(CursedEnergyOutput) × DEFENSE_CE_CAP_FACTOR     (= × 0.5)
      ceReinforcement  = min(ceFromPool, reinforcementCap)
      Defense          = round((ceReinforcement × 6 + S(Durability) × 2) / 6)

    Pipeline: incoming damage → DEFENSIVE move reduction (block) → Defense → final.
    Key clarifications:
      - Defense IS a combat stat (computed, not a stored base stat). "Defense
        stat" is acceptable shorthand for the computed value.
      - CURSED_ENERGY_OUTPUT_INCREASE raises the reinforcement cap.
      - MODIFY_DEFENSE multiplies the effective Defense value.
      - Do NOT confuse with DEFENSIVE (a MoveTag for blocking moves).
      - Do NOT confuse blockDamageReduction (block %) with Defense.

  HIT CHANCE (static):
      hitChance = min(1.0, baseAccuracy × attackerAccuracy
                              / (attackerAccuracy + defenderEvasion × HIT_BALANCE_FACTOR))
    Equal ACC/EVA on a 1.0-base-accuracy move → 95%.

================================================================================
  4. MOVE STRUCTURE  (Move.java, MoveData.java)
================================================================================

Move is the immutable domain object; MoveData is its JSON DTO. Field names below
are the Move/MoveData field names.

  Identity / classification
    id                    6-digit zero-padded string (e.g. "000003"). Auto-assigned
                          by MoveRepository; resequenced on delete.
    name, description     Display text.
    category              MoveCategory (derived from tags; see §6).
    tags                  Set<MoveTag> — the original data tags, retained for
                          UI, filtering, ability scoping, and round-tripping.
    pool                  MovePool (Combat Arts vs Jujutsu Arts; see §7).

  Combat numbers
    basePower             Combined raw damage number across every hit component.
                          0 for non-attacking moves.
    hitComponents         Ordered damage instances. Each stores basePower, its own
                          damage-type tags, a delayTicks offset from unleash,
                          requiresPreviousConnection, and avoidable. Legacy moves
                          without this list synthesize one component from basePower.
    baseAccuracy          Fraction [0.0, 1.0] — inherent accuracy before ACC/EVA.
                          1.0 = 100% (still subject to the hit roll).
    neverMiss             If true, the move bypasses the hit roll and always hits.
    apCost                 AP ticks the move occupies on the AP Timeline. The
                          segment spans [startTick, startTick + apCost - 1].
    unleashPoint          Tick within the segment at which the move fires.
                          Range [1, apCost]. 1 = instant (highest priority);
                          == apCost = full charge (fires last).

  Cursed Energy cost
    baseCeCost            CE cost before efficiency scaling.
    hasCeCost             Whether the move has a CE cost. False = no CE cost and
                          CE is omitted from the card. True = any cost from 0 up.
    minCeCost             Floor: efficiency scaling cannot reduce cost below this.
    maxCeCost             Ceiling: efficiency scaling cannot raise cost above this.

  Defensive data (used only when defenseType != NONE; see §8)
    defenseType           NONE / BLOCK / PARRY / DODGE / SHIELD.
    blockStyle            PERCENTAGE / FLAT. Used only when defenseType = BLOCK.
    blockDuration         0 = whole segment (start..end); -1 = end of round;
                          >0 = that many ticks from the unleash point. Shared by
                          BLOCK, PARRY, and DODGE.
    blockAffectedTags     Null = reduces all damage types. Else reduces only
                          attacks whose tags are a subset of this list. (BLOCK.)
    blockDamageReduction  0–100, PERCENTAGE block only. 100 = full negation.
    blockFlatReduction    FLAT block only. Subtracted from final damage (floor 1).
    dodgeChance           0–100%. DODGE only — probability to avoid a matching
                          incoming attack entirely (no damage). Chance-based;
                          not potency-gated.
    dodgeScope            MELEE / RANGED / BOTH. DODGE only — which attack ranges
                          the dodge reacts to.
    parryStaggerTicks     AP ticks to STAGGER the attacker on a successful parry
                          of a non-GUARD_BREAK attack. PARRY only. 0 = no stagger.
    potency               1–5. Gates defence vs attack: a BLOCK/PARRY applies iff
                          its potency ≥ the attack's potency. Always 1 for utility.

  Effects
    onHitEffects          List<StatusEffect> applied to the target on a hit.
                          STAGGER is configured here with an AP-tick duration.
    selfEffects           List<StatusEffect> applied to the user on unleash
                          (hit/miss/block agnostic). May carry a coded action.
    onBlockEffects        List<StatusEffect> applied to the defender when a BLOCK
                          negates/reduces a hit.
    onParryEffects        List<StatusEffect> applied to the defender when a PARRY
                          negates a hit.
    onDodgeEffects        List<StatusEffect> applied to the defender when a DODGE
                          avoids a hit.

  Effect row flavours (MoveData.StatusEffectData / StatusEffect)
    A move-effect row is exactly one of three mutually-exclusive flavours:
      STATUS  — carries a StatusEffectType; applied to a combatant for a duration.
      CODED   — carries codedAbilityKey + codedAction; dispatched to a compiled
                CodedAbilityRuntime instead of applied as a status.
      SUMMON  — carries summonCharacterId (a SHIKIGAMI definition id); enqueues a
                shikigami onto the wielder's team when the row fires (self / on-hit /
                on-defense). Materialized after the current tick batch, so the summon
                joins planning next round. Only SHIKIGAMI definitions may be summoned.

  Area of effect (MoveTag.AOE + AoeType)
    aoeType               Authoritative AOE targeting shape for an AOE-tagged move
                          (AoeType.java): MULTIPLE / ALL_ENEMIES / ALL_OTHERS. Defaults
                          to ALL_ENEMIES when blank; FRIENDLY_FIRE tag migrates to
                          ALL_OTHERS. Drives MoveTargeting (NONE / SINGLE_ENEMY /
                          MULTIPLE_ENEMIES / ALL_ENEMIES / ALL_OTHERS). AOE bypasses
                          dodge (snapshot taken at fire time; later summons excluded).
    aoeTargetCount        MULTIPLE only — maximum selected enemies hit (min 2).
                          Planning requires 1..N distinct active enemies. Invalid
                          targets are refilled in stable roster order at fire time,
                          but never beyond the number originally selected.

  Learning / gating
    prerequisites         Map<stat name → minimum value>. All must be met to
                          learn and use the move.
    requiredTechniqueId   Innate technique name the character must possess.
                          Null/blank = no restriction. (Innate-tech-tagged moves
                          must set this and a cursedTechniqueMastery prerequisite;
                          non-innate-tech-tagged moves must declare a jujutsuSkill
                          prerequisite — enforced by the Move builder.)
    isFreeMove            If true, the move does not consume a move slot. Still
                          respects prerequisites, technique restrictions, and CE/AP.
    mustBeGranted         If true, the move cannot be assigned directly in the
                          Character Editor; it must be made available through a
                          GRANT_MOVE or FORCE_MOVE ability effect.
    weaponRequired        If true, only a character with a weapon
                          (CharacterData.hasWeapon) can learn/use the move. Forced
                          on for PARRY moves.

  Modifier flags (back dedicated MoveTag entries; see §5)
    stun        boolean  → Move.isStun()       → STUN tag
    guardBreak  boolean  → Move.isGuardBreak() → GUARD_BREAK tag
    heavy       boolean  → Move.isHeavy()      → HEAVY tag

  Other accessors worth knowing:
    isBlackFlashEligible()  true when any individual hit component is eligible.
    isMelee() / isRanged()  derived from the presence of the MELEE/RANGED tags.
    isDefensive()           derived from category == DEFENSIVE.
    isBlock() / isParry() / isDodge()  derived from defenseType.
    isActiveDefense()       true for BLOCK/PARRY/DODGE (has a tracked window).
    dodgeAppliesTo(move)   DODGE scope vs an incoming attack's range.
    parryStaggersAttacker(move)  true iff a parry should stagger the attacker.
    coveredByBlockTags(tags)  true if the attacker's tags are a subset of a
                              block's blockAffectedTags (block-coverage check).
    applyBlockTo(value)      applies a block's reduction to a damage value.

================================================================================
  5. MOVE TAGS  (MoveTag.java)
================================================================================

Tags classify a move. Multiple tags combine; category and pool are derived from
the tag set at runtime. The canonical terms for describing a move's type are the
tag names below.

  TYPE TAGS (select the Power formula / MoveCategory — MoveTag.TYPE_TAGS):
    PHYSICAL              Pure physical strike (fists, weapons, body).
                          Governed by Strength + Combat Ability.
    CURSED_ENERGY         Raw cursed energy without a specific technique. May
                          stand alone as a dedicated CURSED_ENERGY category, or
                          combine with PHYSICAL / technique tags.
                          Governed by CE Output, CE Reserves, CE Efficiency.
    INNATE_TECHNIQUE      Move tied to a specific innate cursed technique
                          (e.g. Miracles, Ratio). Requires requiredTechniqueId.
                          Governed by the CE component + Cursed Technique Mastery.
    NON_INNATE_TECHNIQUE  Move from a learned, non-innate technique (barriers,
                          RCT, cursed-tool styles such as New Shadow Style).
                          Governed by the CE component + Jujutsu Skill.

  FUNCTION TAGS:
    ATTACK                Marks an offensive action. Applied alongside a damage
                          type tag. Non-ATTACK moves prompt for no power/accuracy
                          in the editor.
    UTILITY               Non-damaging move (buffs, marks, resource moves). Not
                          slot-gated by category; counted under Jujutsu Arts
                          unless it carries PHYSICAL.
    DEFENSIVE             A move that reduces or blocks incoming damage
                          (PERCENTAGE_BLOCK / FLAT_BLOCK). Applied BEFORE Defense
                          in the damage pipeline.

  RANGE TAGS (MoveTag.RANGE_TAGS — meaningful only on ATTACK moves):
    MELEE                 Close-quarters strike. Modifier tag: does not affect
                          Power, category, or Black Flash eligibility. Lives only
                          in the tag set (no dedicated Move flag).
    RANGED                Distance strike (projectile, beam, thrown weapon).
                          Same rules as MELEE.

  WEAPON TAG:
    SWORD                 Sword / sword-like weapon modifier used by move-scoped
                           abilities (e.g. Earnest Swordswoman). Modifier tag.

  MODIFIER FLAGS (also backed by a dedicated boolean on Move):
    STUN                  On a successful offensive hit, the defender's action
                          segment(s) active on the current tick are stunned
                          (removed from the timeline and prevented from firing).
                          Segments that already fired are unaffected; HEAVY
                           segments resist the stun. Defensive/utility moves do
                           not apply the stun effect. Backed by Move.isStun().
    GUARD_BREAK           A successful hit ignores the defender's BLOCK defensive
                          moves — the block applies no reduction and cannot negate
                          the hit. Dodges and parries are unaffected; only blocks
                          bypass. A guard-break attack is still negated by a parry
                          but does NOT get staggered by one. Backed by
                          Move.isGuardBreak().
    HEAVY                 An action segment carrying this move is IMMUNE to being
                          stunned by a STUN-tagged hit. HEAVY only resists the
                           STUN tag (not CE-depletion, ability-driven stuns, or
                           the STAGGER status).
                          The "heavy beats stun" half of the stun ↔ heavy
                          interaction. Backed by Move.isHeavy().

  Black Flash eligibility rule (evaluated per hit component): a component must
    contain both PHYSICAL and CURSED_ENERGY, and must not contain
    INNATE_TECHNIQUE or NON_INNATE_TECHNIQUE.

================================================================================
  6. MOVE CATEGORIES  (MoveCategory.java)
================================================================================

Categories are derived at runtime from the move's tag set (exact set match via
MoveCategory.fromTags). They determine the Power formula and Black Flash
eligibility. Pool (slot budget) is derived separately from the raw tags (§7).

  Category                                Tags                              BF?
  PHYSICAL                                {PHYSICAL}                        No
  CURSED_ENERGY                           {CURSED_ENERGY}                   No   (raw CE attack)
  INNATE_TECHNIQUE                        {INNATE_TECHNIQUE}                No
  NON_INNATE_TECHNIQUE                    {NON_INNATE_TECHNIQUE}            No
  PHYSICAL_CURSED_ENERGY                  {PHYSICAL, CURSED_ENERGY}         Yes
  PHYSICAL_INNATE_TECHNIQUE               {PHYSICAL, INNATE_TECHNIQUE}      No
  PHYSICAL_NON_INNATE_TECHNIQUE           {PHYSICAL, NON_INNATE_TECHNIQUE}  No
  INNATE_NON_INNATE_TECHNIQUE             {INNATE_TECHNIQUE, NON_INNATE_TECHNIQUE}  No
  PHYSICAL_INNATE_NON_INNATE_TECHNIQUE    {all three technique tags}        No
  UTILITY                                 {UTILITY}                         No
  DEFENSIVE                               {DEFENSIVE}                       No

  Black Flash eligible (the only category with PHYSICAL + CURSED_ENERGY and
  no technique tag): PHYSICAL_CURSED_ENERGY.

  MoveData.derivedCategory() collapses DEFENSIVE and UTILITY first (regardless of
  any other tags) and falls back to UTILITY for unknown combinations.

================================================================================
  7. MOVE POOLS & MOVE SLOTS  (MovePool.java, SlotBudgetEnforcer.java)
================================================================================

POOL answers one question: which stat's slot budget does this move draw from?
Pool is orthogonal to category and to tactical function (offensive/defensive/
utility). It is derived from the RAW tag set, not from MoveCategory, because
MoveCategory collapses the PHYSICAL tag away for defensive/utility moves.

  COMBAT_ARTS    Moves carrying the PHYSICAL tag.  Slots ← Combat Ability (raw).
  JUJUTSU_ARTS   Moves WITHOUT the PHYSICAL tag.   Slots ← Jujutsu Skill (raw).

  Hybrid rule: any move containing PHYSICAL counts as a Combat Art, even if it
  also carries technique or CE tags. A non-PHYSICAL move is always a Jujutsu Art
  regardless of whether it is offensive, defensive, or utility.

MOVE SLOTS
  The number of moves of each pool a character can equip. Progression is a
  threshold table (ART_SLOT_TIERS), shared by both pools, read off the RAW
  governing stat — slots are the one derived value that BYPASSES StatScale so
  thresholds land on clean stat milestones instead of distorted numbers.

  stat ≥  :   0   30   45   60   80   100   120   150   180   210   240   270   300
  slots   :   2    3    4    5    6     8     9    10    11    12    13    14    15

  In words: everyone gets 2 of each to start, then +1 at 30/45/60/80, +2 at 100,
  +1 at 120/150, then +1 every 30 points up to a max of 15 at 300. Stats above
  the max tier clamp to 15.
  Governing stats: COMBAT_ABILITY for Combat Arts, JUJUTSU_SKILL for Jujutsu Arts.
  (Cursed Technique Mastery does NOT grant slots.)
  Free moves (isFreeMove) skip only the slot-budget check.

================================================================================
  8. DEFENSE TYPES  (DefenseType.java, BlockStyle.java, DodgeScope.java)
================================================================================

A DEFENSIVE move picks one defense type. Three are implemented (BLOCK, PARRY,
DODGE); SHIELD is reserved.

Shared duration rules (blockDuration; same for BLOCK, PARRY, DODGE):
    blockDuration = 0    defense lasts the whole segment (start..endTick).
    blockDuration = -1   defense lasts until end of round.
    blockDuration > 0    defense lasts that many ticks from the unleash point.

POTENCY GATE — attack vs defence
  Every attack and every BLOCK/PARRY move carries a potency (1–5). A BLOCK or
  PARRY applies iff its potency ≥ the incoming attack's potency; a higher-potency
  attack blows through a lower-potency defence (the defence is simply ignored).
  DODGE is chance-based and ignores potency. The gate is checked in
  DamageCalculator; the window itself is tracked by Timeline.activeDefenseAt.

GUARD_BREAK interaction
  GUARD_BREAK bypasses BLOCK only. PARRY and DODGE are unaffected: a guard-break
  attack is still parried (negated) and still rolled against by a dodge. A parry
  of a guard-break attack deals no damage but does NOT stagger the attacker.

NONE
  Not a defensive move. No defence applied.

BLOCK
  Damage reduction. The reduction formula is set by BlockStyle:
    PERCENTAGE  attack value × (1 − blockDamageReduction/100).
                blockDamageReduction = 100  → full negation (outcome BLOCKED).
                blockDamageReduction < 100  → partial (outcome MOVE_BLOCK_REDUCED).
    FLAT        subtract blockFlatReduction (damage floor 1).
  Applied BEFORE Defense in the damage pipeline. Do NOT call "blockDamageReduction"
  the "Defense" value — Defense is the computed stat applied AFTER the block.
  blockAffectedTags: null/empty = all damage types; else only attacks whose tags
  are a subset of the list (Move.coveredByBlockTags).
  On a block, onBlockEffects fire on the defender.

PARRY
  A short reactive window that negates a hit entirely (outcome PARRIED, no damage
  roll). Requires a weapon: weaponRequired is forced on for parries, so only a
  character with a weapon (CharacterData.hasWeapon) can use one. Parries are
  weapon-based and type-agnostic — any ATTACK can be parried.
  On a successful parry of a non-GUARD_BREAK attack, the attacker is STAGGERED
  for parryStaggerTicks AP ticks (0 = no stagger). A GUARD_BREAK attack is still
  negated but is NOT staggered.
  On a parry, onParryEffects fire on the defender.

DODGE
  While active, each incoming attack matching the dodgeScope has a dodgeChance%
  probability to be avoided entirely (outcome DODGED, no damage). Type-agnostic
  (works vs physical and cursed energy) and NOT potency-gated. AOE attacks
  (MoveTag.AOE) bypass dodge. dodgeScope (DodgeScope): MELEE / RANGED / BOTH.
  On a dodge, onDodgeEffects fire on the defender.

SHIELD
  Reserved for a future defence flavour. Not yet implemented — the editor exposes
  it as a placeholder and the engine treats it as NONE.

DEFENSE DIALOGUE
  Each type has its own activation/expiry and outcome lines:
    BLOCK activation: "X raises their block! (…)"   expiry: "X drops their guard!"
    PARRY activation: "X readies a parry!"           expiry: "X lowers their parry stance."
    DODGE activation: "X stands ready to dodge!"     expiry: "X drops their ready stance."
  Outcomes (distinct events): MOVE_BLOCKED / MOVE_BLOCK_REDUCED, MOVE_PARRIED,
  MOVE_DODGED — note DODGED ("X dodged Y!") is separate from MOVE_MISSED
  ("Y missed X!").

================================================================================
  9. AP TIMELINE  (Timeline.java, ActionSegment.java, BattlePlan.java)
================================================================================

AP BAR
  The total action points a combatant has per round (Max AP, §3). Filled with
  action segments during planning.

AP TIMELINE
  The ordered sequence of action segments queued for a round, packed from tick 1.
  Empty space between segments is valid (idle ticks). Two boards exist per plan:
  an offensive board and a defensive board; they are merged for legacy resolution.

TICK (a.k.a. AP tick / game tick)
  One unit of the timeline. The action counter advances tick by tick during
  resolution. DEFAULT_GRID_LENGTH = 150; ticks are 1-indexed (1..gridLength).

ACTION COUNTER
  The sweeping counter that advances tick by tick during RESOLUTION. When it
  reaches a segment's startTick: CE is drained. When it reaches its fireTick: the
  move fires.

ACTION SEGMENT
  One move's occupancy on the timeline.
    startTick      CE is drained here.
    fireTick       = startTick + unleashPoint − 1. The move's effect resolves here.
    endTick        = startTick + apCost − 1. The last tick of the segment.
    actualCeCost   CE after efficiency scaling (drained at startTick).
    stunned / fired flags.
  Can be stunned (skipped) by: a STUN-tagged hit, CE depletion, or an ability
  cancellation. markFired() is final — a fired segment cannot be retro-cancelled
  (a fired block keeps protecting for its AP window).

INSTANT MOVE
  A move with unleashPoint == 1 (fireTick == startTick). Highest priority in
  tie-breaking — fires before other same-tick moves; further ties broken by Speed.
  This speed ordering applies to defenses too: a defense only contests an attack
  that lands on its tick if it has ALREADY fired (i.e. it won the same-tick
  speed ordering). A slower same-tick defense has not fired yet when a faster
  attack resolves, so it does NOT contest — defending means committing the block
  to fire BEFORE the attack lands, not merely on the same tick.

STUNNED (segment state)
  An action segment removed from firing — by a STUN-tagged hit, by CE depletion,
  or by an ability cancellation (CANCEL_NEXT_MOVE). Its AP slot is still spent
  and any CE already drained remains spent; the move simply does not fire.
  Log line: "<combatant> was stunned and could not move."

================================================================================
  10. COMBAT PHASES  (BattleState.java)
================================================================================

  Phase enum: PLANNING, RESOLUTION, ROUND_END, BATTLE_OVER.
  Cycle: PLANNING → RESOLUTION → ROUND_END → PLANNING … → BATTLE_OVER.

PLANNING
  Both combatants submit their move queues (AP timelines) for the round. The
  player selects manually; the AI (AIStrategy) selects automatically. Automatic
  statuses timed ROUND_START refresh here.

RESOLUTION
  currentTick starts at 1 and advances tick by tick. Moves fire, damage is
  resolved, stun effects and coded effects are applied.

ROUND END (round maintenance)
  Status effects tick, Black Flash State expiry is checked (tickBfsExpiry),
  defense buffs and block states are cleared, roundNumber increments,
  currentTick resets to 0, ROUND_START automatic statuses re-apply.

BATTLE OVER
  One or both combatants have 0 HP. Winner is declared. Simultaneous KO = draw
  (winner is null). checkAndResolveBattleOver sets the phase when either
  combatant isDefeated().

ROUND
  One full cycle of PLANNING → RESOLUTION → ROUND END. roundNumber starts at 1.
  Automatic statuses also fire at FIGHT_START (construction) then each ROUND_START.

================================================================================
  11. DAMAGE CALCULATION  (DamageCalculator.java, PowerCalculator.java)
================================================================================

Pipeline:
  1. HIT ROLL        — does the move connect? (neverMiss / guaranteed hit /
                       guaranteed dodge / hitChance roll)
  2. CODED CONNECT   — onAttackConnected runs on a confirmed connection only.
  3. GUARD_BREAK     — if forceFullBlock and !move.isGuardBreak() → BLOCKED;
                       bypassBlock = move.isGuardBreak() || codedModifiers.bypassBlock().
  4. BLOCK REDUCTION — active DEFENSIVE move reduces attackValue (skipped if bypassBlock).
  5. POWER           — PowerCalculator.compute(category, attackerStats), then run
                       through attacker.modifyBattleStat(POWER).
  6. DEFENSE         — max(1, defender.computeCurrentDefense(tick) × codedModifiers.defenseMultiplier()).
  7. DAMAGE FORMULA  — scale, roll, and damage multipliers applied (see below).
  8. BLACK FLASH     — BF roll on eligible moves; ×2.5 if proc'd.

  DAMAGE FORMULA (verbatim, DamageCalculator):
    power       = PowerCalculator.compute(category, acs)
    if category == PHYSICAL: power ×= PHYSICAL_POWER_MULTIPLIER  (0.85)
    power       = attacker.modifyBattleStat(POWER, power)
    attackValue = basePower × basePowerMultiplierFor(move) × power
    attackValueAfterBlock = block applied to attackValue (if any active block)
    defense     = max(1, defender.computeCurrentDefense(tick) × codedModifiers.defenseMultiplier())
    randomRoll  = ROLL_MIN + (1 − ROLL_MIN) × rng.nextDouble()     // ROLL_MIN = 0.85
    rawDamage   = round( (attackValueAfterBlock / defense)
                         × DAMAGE_SCALE × randomRoll
                         × attacker.getAbilityFlags().damageMultiplierFor(move) )
    rawDamage   = max(1, rawDamage)                                 // floor 1 on a hit
    rawDamage   = attacker.modifyBattleStat(DAMAGE_DEALT, rawDamage)
    finalDamage = rawDamage  (× BF_DAMAGE_MULTIPLIER if Black Flash proc'd)

    NOTE: power and defense are computed from StatScale-scaled stats (see STAT
    SCALING above). PHYSICAL Power is dampened by PHYSICAL_POWER_MULTIPLIER so a
    physical Power edge cannot two-hit a peer; CE/technique moves are unaffected.

  DAMAGE_SCALE = 0.42  (private, tuned for longer fights; was 0.5).
  DAMAGE ROLL range = [0.85, 1.0] (±15%).

POWER (PowerCalculator — integer division; cursedEnergyBase is shared;
       every stat input is StatScale-scaled, S(stat)):
  PHYSICAL                            (S(Strength) × 4 + S(CombatAbility)) / 5
  CURSED_ENERGY (base)                (S(CE_Output) × 3 + S(CE_Reserves) × 2 + S(CE_Efficiency)) / 6
  INNATE_TECHNIQUE                    (cursedEnergyBase + S(CursedTechniqueMastery)) / 2
  NON_INNATE_TECHNIQUE                (cursedEnergyBase + S(JujutsuSkill)) / 2
  PHYSICAL_CURSED_ENERGY              (cursedEnergyBase × 3 + physical) / 4
  PHYSICAL_INNATE_TECHNIQUE           (innateTechnique × 4 + physical) / 5
  PHYSICAL_NON_INNATE_TECHNIQUE       (nonInnateTechnique × 3 + physical) / 4
  INNATE_NON_INNATE_TECHNIQUE         (innateTechnique × 3 + nonInnateTechnique × 2) / 5
  PHYSICAL_INNATE_NON_INNATE_TECHNIQUE(physical + innateTechnique × 3 + nonInnateTechnique × 2) / 6
  UTILITY, DEFENSIVE                  0

OUTCOMES (DamageResult.Outcome):
  MISS      Hit roll failed (or guaranteed dodge). No damage.
  DODGED    A live DODGE defence avoided the attack (dodgeChance roll). No damage.
  PARRIED   A live PARRY negated the attack (potency-gated). No damage; the
            attacker may be STAGGERED if the move lacked GUARD_BREAK.
  BLOCKED   Connected but fully negated by a BLOCK (PERCENTAGE at 100%).
  HIT       Connected. Final damage applied (may include partial block reduction).

RESOLUTION ORDER (DamageCalculator.resolve):
  dodge roll (DODGE, chance-based) → hit roll → coded onAttackConnected →
  parry check (PARRY, potency-gated) → forceFullBlock → block query (BLOCK,
  potency-gated; bypassed by GUARD_BREAK) → power → block reduction →
  defense → damage → Black Flash.

RAW DAMAGE     Damage before the Black Flash multiplier.
FINAL DAMAGE   Damage after all modifiers (block reduction, BF multiplier). What is
               subtracted from the defender's HP.

================================================================================
  12. BLACK FLASH  (CombatStats.java, BattleCombatant.java, DamageCalculator.java)
================================================================================

BLACK FLASH (BF)
  A rare critical hit that multiplies damage, rolled only on BF-eligible moves
  (the four PHYSICAL + CE-bearing categories, §6). BF_DAMAGE_MULTIPLIER = ×2.5,
  applied to raw damage.

BLACK FLASH CHANCE
  Base BF_BASE_CHANCE = 3% (0.03). Modified by BF_CHANCE_ADD and by BFS escalation.

BLACK FLASH STATE (BFS)
  A temporary state entered after a Black Flash proc. Each consecutive BF hit
  while in BFS escalates the BF chance for subsequent moves:
    Normal (not in BFS)                 3%   (BF_BASE_CHANCE)
    BFS, 0 consecutive hits             10%  (BFS_BF_CHANCES[0])
    BFS, 1 consecutive hit              20%  (BFS_BF_CHANCES[1])
    BFS, 2 consecutive hits             35%  (BFS_BF_CHANCES[2])
    BFS, 3+ consecutive hits            50%  (cap, BFS_BF_CHANCES[3])

  State machine (BattleCombatant):
    enterBlackFlashState(round)  on first entry sets inBlackFlashState=true and
                                 resets consecutiveBfsHits=0; always sets
                                 bfsExpiresAfterRound = round + 1 (refreshed each proc).
    recordBfsHit()               increments consecutiveBfsHits (called AFTER
                                 enterBlackFlashState on a re-trigger).
    tickBfsExpiry(completedRound) clears BFS when completedRound ≥ bfsExpiresAfterRound.
    getCurrentBfChance()         returns the array entry if in BFS else BF_BASE_CHANCE,
                                 + bfChanceBonus, through modifyBattleStat(BLACK_FLASH_CHANCE),
                                 capped at 1.0.

  On each BF proc: CE restored = round(maxCE × BF_CE_RESTORE_FRACTION) = 5% of max CE.
  BFS expiry is checked at ROUND_END and emits a BFS_EXPIRED event.

================================================================================
  13. CURSED ENERGY  (CharacterStats.java, CombatStats.java)
================================================================================

CURSED ENERGY (CE)
  A combat resource. Spent when a CE-tagged action segment reaches its startTick.
  Not regenerated during a fight (except BF procs and ability effects). Defense
  holds at a plateau while the pool can supply the CE Output cap; once the pool
  drops below that cap, Defense degrades.

CE POOL (Max Cursed Energy)
  Total CE at fight start. CE Pool = round(S(CursedEnergyReserves) × CE_POOL_SCALE) (= ×8, scaled).

CE COST (of a move)
  The CE drained when a CE-tagged segment reaches its startTick. Scaled by CE
  Efficiency from baseCeCost, clamped to [minCeCost, maxCeCost]. Abilities can
  force minimum (CE_COST_TO_MINIMUM) or multiply (CE_COST_MULTIPLY).

CE EFFICIENCY SCALING
  actualCost = baseCeCost × (80 / cursedEnergyEfficiency).
  Efficiency 80 = neutral. Above 80 = cheaper. Below 80 = costlier.

================================================================================
  14. ABILITIES  (AbilityEffectType.java, AbilityApplicator.java)
================================================================================

ABILITY
  A passive (or, in data, ACTIVE) power attached to a character. Each ability has
  a category (PASSIVE / ACTIVE), a sourceType (CHARACTER / TECHNIQUE / MOVE /
  STAT_THRESHOLD / ABILITY), an optional innate-technique mastery threshold, and a
  list of effects. Mechanics that require compiled code are represented by an
  editable CODED effect row inside that list (see §15); the ability remains an
  ordinary mutable data wrapper and may combine coded and generic effects.

PASSIVE ABILITY
  Applied once at BattleCombatant construction via AbilityApplicator for
  always-active effects, which persist for the entire fight. Triggered runtime
  effects (HEAL_HP and below) are instead executed by the passive dispatcher when
  the ability's activation predicate fires.

ACTIVE ABILITY
  Linked to a move (activeMoveId) and normalized to subtype QUEUED on save; its
  effects list is cleared. (Combat hook-up for queued actives is incomplete.)

AVAILABLE
  Content shown in the Character Editor's AVAILABLE list. Available content is
  not learned or active until moved into LEARNED (moves) or ASSIGNED (abilities).

GRANT_MOVE        Adds a move to AVAILABLE. The move must still be learned
                  normally (prerequisites, technique, slot budget all apply).
GRANT_ABILITY     Adds an ability to AVAILABLE for normal assignment.
FORCE_MOVE        Adds a move directly to LEARNED and locks it: it bypasses
                  normal prerequisites and slot limits, is greyed in the editor,
                  and cannot be removed while its source remains active. Source
                  label: "Granted by ability" / "Granted by technique".
UNLOCK_TECHNIQUE  Lets a character learn and use moves belonging to a technique
                  it does not innately possess.

CHARACTER-SOURCE ABILITY
  An ability whose sourceType is CHARACTER. It is available to every character;
  CHARACTER does not mean character-exclusive. Only assigned abilities are active.

ABILITY EFFECT TYPES  (53 enum constants; grouped as in source).
  Each type owns its editor display name, description, required parameters, and
  defaults. Effects with ordinal ≥ HEAL_HP are TRIGGERED RUNTIME EFFECTS: applied
  by the passive dispatcher on activation, not at construction.

  Stat modifiers (construction-time):
    STAT_ADD            Add to stat                       (STAT, INTEGER)
    STAT_MULTIPLY       Multiply stat (after additive)    (STAT, DECIMAL)
    STAT_DIVIDE         Divide stat (after additive)      (STAT, DECIMAL)
    STAT_SET_VALUE      Set stat to an exact value        (STAT, INTEGER)
    STAT_SET_MIN        Set stat to 0 (display N/A)       (STAT)
    STAT_ALLOCATION_MINIMUM  Set editor allocation floor  (STAT, INTEGER)
    STAT_BONUS_POINTS   Change point-buy budget (editor)  (INTEGER)

  Marker templates:
    POISON_IMMUNITY     Mark the character as poison-immune
    SOUL_AWARE_ATTACKS  Mark the character's attacks as soul-aware

  Compiled mechanics:
    CODED               Enable an allow-listed compiled feature  (CODED_FEATURE)

  CE cost modifiers (move-scoped):
    CE_COST_TO_MINIMUM  Force matching moves to min CE cost      (MOVE_SCOPE)
    CE_COST_MULTIPLY    Multiply CE cost of matching moves        (MOVE_SCOPE, DECIMAL)

  Accuracy modifiers (move-scoped):
    MOVE_ACCURACY_ADD         ± accuracy on own moves           (MOVE_SCOPE, INTEGER)
    MOVE_ACCURACY_MULTIPLY    × accuracy on own moves           (MOVE_SCOPE, DECIMAL)
    OPPONENT_ACCURACY_ADD     ± accuracy on enemy's moves       (MOVE_SCOPE, INTEGER)
    OPPONENT_ACCURACY_MULTIPLY × accuracy on enemy's moves      (MOVE_SCOPE, DECIMAL)

  Damage modifiers (move-scoped):
    DAMAGE_MULTIPLY           × damage dealt                   (MOVE_SCOPE, DECIMAL)
    MOVE_BASE_POWER_MULTIPLY  × configured base power (pre-Power, pre-Defense) (MOVE_SCOPE, DECIMAL)

  Grants / technique:
    GRANT_MOVE         Add move to AVAILABLE                    (MOVE_ID)
    GRANT_ABILITY      Add ability to AVAILABLE                 (ABILITY_ID)
    FORCE_MOVE         Add+lock move in LEARNED                 (MOVE_ID)
    UNLOCK_TECHNIQUE   Grant an innate cursed technique         (TECHNIQUE)

  Other persistent passives:
    BF_CHANCE_ADD      ± Black Flash chance (enter 5 for +5pp)  (DECIMAL)
    MODIFY_DEFENSE     × effective Defense                       (DECIMAL)
    MODIFY_AP_BAR      ± flat AP bar size                        (INTEGER)
    AUTO_STATUS_APPLY  Apply a status automatically              (STATUS_TYPE, TARGET, TIMING, DURATION, MAGNITUDE)
    LOCK_MOVE_TAG      Prevent this character using one tag      (MOVE_SCOPE)
    COST_CE_PER_ROUND  Drain CE at round start                   (INTEGER)

  Triggered runtime — resources (target SELF / ENEMY / BOTH):
    HEAL_HP            Restore flat HP                           (TARGET, INTEGER)
    HEAL_HP_PERCENT    Restore % of max HP                       (TARGET, DECIMAL)
    RESTORE_CE         Restore flat CE                           (TARGET, INTEGER)
    RESTORE_CE_PERCENT Restore % of max CE                       (TARGET, DECIMAL)
    DRAIN_CE           Remove flat CE                            (TARGET, INTEGER)
    DRAIN_CE_PERCENT   Remove % of max CE                        (TARGET, DECIMAL)
    DEAL_DIRECT_DAMAGE Fixed damage, bypasses accuracy/defense   (TARGET, INTEGER)
    DEAL_MAX_HP_DAMAGE % of target max HP as direct damage       (TARGET, DECIMAL)
    INSTANT_KILL       Reduce to 0 HP (unless fatal protection)  (TARGET)

  Triggered runtime — status control:
    APPLY_STATUS       Apply any status                          (STATUS_TYPE, TARGET, DURATION, MAGNITUDE)
    REMOVE_STATUS      Remove every instance of one status       (STATUS_TYPE, TARGET)
    CLEAR_STATUSES     Remove every active status                (TARGET)

  Triggered runtime — timed (TEMP_*) stat/battle effects:
    TEMP_STAT_ADD      Timed ± to a base stat                    (STAT, TARGET, INTEGER, DURATION)
    TEMP_STAT_MULTIPLY Timed × a base stat                       (STAT, TARGET, DECIMAL, DURATION)
    TEMP_STAT_SET_VALUE Timed set of a base stat                 (STAT, TARGET, INTEGER, DURATION)
    BATTLE_STAT_ADD    Timed ± to a battle stat                  (BATTLE_STAT, TARGET, DECIMAL, DURATION)
    BATTLE_STAT_MULTIPLY Timed × a battle stat                   (BATTLE_STAT, TARGET, DECIMAL, DURATION)

  Triggered runtime — one-shot defensive/offensive guarantees (USES-based):
    IGNORE_DAMAGE           Negate damaging instances (USES=1 once, −1 every hit) (TARGET, USES, DURATION)
    DAMAGE_SHIELD           Absorb up to a flat total of incoming damage          (TARGET, INTEGER, DURATION)
    SURVIVE_FATAL_DAMAGE    Leave target at 1 HP when damage would defeat them    (TARGET, USES, DURATION)
    GUARANTEE_NEXT_HIT      Target's next eligible attack hits                    (TARGET, USES, DURATION)
    GUARANTEE_NEXT_DODGE    Target dodges the next attack against them            (TARGET, USES, DURATION)
    GUARANTEE_NEXT_BLACK_FLASH  Target's next BF-eligible hit becomes a Black Flash (TARGET, USES, DURATION)
    CANCEL_NEXT_MOVE        Stun the target's next move when it begins            (TARGET, USES, DURATION)
    TEMP_LOCK_MOVE_TAG      Prevent target planning moves with one tag            (TARGET, MOVE_SCOPE, DURATION)

  Effect parameters (AbilityEffectParameter): STAT, INTEGER, DECIMAL, MOVE_SCOPE,
    MOVE_ID, ABILITY_ID, TECHNIQUE, STATUS_TYPE, TARGET, TIMING, DURATION,
    MAGNITUDE, USES, BATTLE_STAT, CODED_FEATURE.
  Effect target (AbilityEffectTarget): SELF, ENEMY, BOTH.
  Effect timing (AbilityEffectTiming): FIGHT_START, ROUND_START, ON_HIT.
    A ROUND_START AUTO_STATUS_APPLY must last exactly 1 round / 0 ticks so it
    refreshes without stacking.

  Per-stat processing order (AbilityApplicator):
    STAT_SET_MIN / STAT_SET_VALUE (overrides) → STAT_ADD (additive) →
    STAT_MULTIPLY / STAT_DIVIDE (multiplicative, product) → noNeg clamp (≥0).
  Move-scoped effects filter by tag: appliesTo(effect, move) is true when the
  effect's moveTag is blank OR move.hasTag(moveTag).

================================================================================
  15. CODED ABILITIES  (coded/*.java, CodedAbilityRegistry.java)
================================================================================

Some mechanics cannot be composed from generic effect types. These are
implemented as COMPILED CODED ABILITIES and exposed in data through effect rows:
an ability uses an AbilityEffectData row with type CODED, codedAbilityKey, and
codedFeature; a move uses a self/on-hit effect carrying codedAbilityKey and
codedAction. Moves and abilities stay editable data wrappers, while the hardcoded
behaviour lives in a CodedAbilityRuntime selected by the effect row.

The registry (CodedAbilityRegistry) is an allow-list. At combatant construction
it collects every coded ability key the character has (from CODED rows on assigned
abilities and from coded effect rows on known moves), validates each binding, and
instantiates one runtime per key. Unknown bindings log a warning and are skipped.

CodedAbilityRuntime hooks (all optional except the first two):
  onTrigger(state, trigger)            activation predicate fired
  onEffectFired(state, effect, attacker, defender, tick)  coded effect row fired
  onAttackConnected(attacker, defender, move, tick, rng)  → CodedHitModifiers (pre-block/defense)
  beforeIncomingMove(state, attacker, defender, move, tick) → CodedMoveResponse
  tickTimelineEffects(tick)            advance universal-tick timers
  preventFatalDamage()                 true if a fatal hit should be negated this tick
  drainPendingEvents(tick)             flush queued events
  state()                              → CodedAbilityState snapshot for the view

Shipped coded abilities (keys / features / actions):

  MIRACLES                              (MiraclesAbility)
    KEY = "MIRACLES". Legacy defaults remain six; coded effect parameters and
    CTM progression define the live capacity and starting amount.
    Features:
      RESERVOIR          Gain a configured starting amount; capacity scales with CTM.
      FATEFUL_REPRIEVE   Expend 1 Miracle to negate fatal damage (preventFatalDamage).
      FORTUNE_RECLAIMED  Gain 1 Miracle when the enemy misses or on your Black Flash.
    Effect action: CREATE — a coded self-effect that creates a Miracle on unleash
                   (used by the Miracle Creation move).

  RATIO                                 (RatioAbility)
    KEY = "RATIO". Legacy defaults are 3 stacks, 50 ticks, 70% trigger chance,
    and 30% Defense. Coded parameters and CTM progression define live values.
    Features:
      REINFORCEMENT_RATIO  Gives connected reinforcement hits a CTM-scaled chance
                           to apply Ratio directly and owns total stack capacity.
      APPLY_TO_MOVE        Apply Ratio directly for the current connected hit.
      CREATE_STACKS        Create target-bound stacks from a coded effect row.
    Effect action: RATIO_EFFECT — a coded effect whose target mode is
                   APPLY_TO_MOVE or CREATE_STACKS. Stack rows configure count,
                   duration, trigger chance, and Defense remaining.

  NEW_SHADOW_STYLE                      (NewShadowStyleAbility)
    KEY = "NEW_SHADOW_STYLE".
    Features:
      SIMPLE_DOMAIN_BINDING_VOW  Using any other move dispels an active Simple Domain.
    Effect action: ACTIVATE_SIMPLE_DOMAIN — a coded self-effect (on the Simple
                   Domain move) that activates Simple Domain as a reaction bound
                   to a target move (codedTarget = move id, e.g. Batto Sword
                   Drawing). Reacts to that move via MoveTag.STUN.

CodedAbilityState (emitted with events) carries {key, label, value, max} for the
view (e.g. "Miracles 3/6", "Ratio 2/3").

Effect actions exposed to editors (CodedAbilityRegistry.effectActions):
  MIRACLES / CREATE                 "Create Miracle"
  RATIO / RATIO_EFFECT              "Ratio Effect"
  NEW_SHADOW_STYLE / ACTIVATE_SIMPLE_DOMAIN  "Activate Simple Domain"

Ability features exposed to editors (CodedAbilityRegistry.abilityFeatures):
  MIRACLES / RESERVOIR                    "Miracle Reservoir"
  MIRACLES / FATEFUL_REPRIEVE             "Fateful Reprieve"
  MIRACLES / FORTUNE_RECLAIMED            "Fortune Reclaimed"
  RATIO / REINFORCEMENT_RATIO             "Ratio Reinforcement"
  NEW_SHADOW_STYLE / SIMPLE_DOMAIN_BINDING_VOW  "Simple Domain Binding Vow"

================================================================================
  16. STATUS EFFECTS  (StatusEffectType.java)
================================================================================

Temporary effects applied to characters by moves (onHitEffects / selfEffects)
or ability effects (AUTO_STATUS_APPLY, APPLY_STATUS, TEMP_STAT_*). Stat effects
have a non-negative magnitude and an explicit direction in the type name
(*_INCREASE adds, *_DECREASE subtracts).

Duration rules (StatusEffect.validateDuration):
  rounds > 0, ticks = 0      round-only
  rounds = 0, ticks > 0      tick-only
  both > 0                   full rounds, then that many ticks of the next round
  rounds = -1, ticks = 0     permanent

Every base stat has both directions (StatKey-backed):
  VITALITY, STRENGTH, DURABILITY, SPEED, COMBAT_ABILITY,
  CURSED_ENERGY_RESERVES, CURSED_ENERGY_EFFICIENCY,
  CURSED_ENERGY_OUTPUT, JUJUTSU_SKILL, CURSED_TECHNIQUE_MASTERY
    → *_INCREASE / *_DECREASE

Every runtime battle stat has both directions (BattleStatKey-backed):
  MAX_HP, MAX_CURSED_ENERGY (MAX_CE), MAX_AP, ACCURACY, EVASION, POWER, DEFENSE
    → *_INCREASE / *_DECREASE

Non-stat control status:
  STAGGER
    Must use rounds = 0 and ticks > 0; its magnitude is unused (stored as 0).
    On the tick it is applied and on every remaining active AP tick, it stuns
    the affected character's active, not-yet-fired action segments. This is a
    character status, not the STUN move tag, so HEAVY does not resist it.

  SLEEP
    Must use rounds > 0 with 0 ticks. When an affected combatant reaches an
    action's fire tick, that action fails and the battle log names the attempted
    move. Damage wakes the combatant immediately and allows later actions;
    otherwise SLEEP expires at the end of the current round.

  POISON
    Must use rounds > 0 with 0 ticks, or be permanent. Magnitude stores flat
    damage per round for future poison resolution.

Total: 17 stat pairs plus STAGGER, SLEEP, and POISON = 37 constants.

Legacy aliases (StatusEffectType.fromName resolves these; "signed" names can mean
either direction depending on the stored magnitude's sign):
  FOCUS → ACCURACY_INCREASE
  CE_OUTPUT_UP → CURSED_ENERGY_OUTPUT_INCREASE
  SPEED_UP → SPEED_INCREASE
  POWER_UP → POWER_INCREASE
  DEFENSE_UP → DEFENSE_INCREASE
  BIND → EVASION_DECREASE
  CURSED_SEAL → CURSED_ENERGY_EFFICIENCY_DECREASE
  AP_DRAIN → MAX_AP_DECREASE
Old fractional Power/Defense/Accuracy magnitudes are multiplied by 100 into flat
stat points (normalizeStoredMagnitude).

================================================================================
  17. COMBAT EVENTS  (CombatEvent.java)
================================================================================

Immutable records emitted by CombatResolver and consumed by the View layer (the
Observer bridge — the core never calls the view directly). These are the
canonical names for what happened on a given tick (tick = 0 for system/round
events). Each event carries source, target, move, intValue, tick, an optional
hit-component index, an optional CodedAbilityState snapshot, and a message.

  Move execution:
    MOVE_FIRED          move unleashed — attack resolved
    MOVE_MISSED         hit roll failed
    MOVE_BLOCKED        block fully negated the damage (PERCENTAGE_BLOCK at 100%)
    MOVE_BLOCK_REDUCED  block reduced but did not fully negate damage
    MOVE_STUNNED        a STUN tag, active STAGGER status, CE depletion, or
                         ability cancellation removed this action segment

  Damage:
    DAMAGE_DEALT        HP was deducted from a combatant
    DAMAGE_IGNORED      incoming damage was negated (IGNORE_DAMAGE / shield)
    HP_RESTORED         HP was restored
    MAX_HP_CHANGED      max HP changed (battle stat effect)
    MAX_CE_CHANGED      max CE changed (battle stat effect)
    BLACK_FLASH         BF proc; includes the BF damage value

  CE:
    CE_DRAINED          CE was spent on a move
    CE_RESTORED         CE was returned (e.g. BF proc restores 5%, RESTORE_CE)
    CE_DEPLETED         combatant reached 0 CE

  Status:
    STATUS_APPLIED      a status effect or block message was applied
    STATUS_EXPIRED      a status effect ended

  BFS:
    BFS_EXPIRED         Black Flash State ended (round maintenance)

  Abilities:
    ABILITY_ACTIVATED   a triggered/coded ability activated

  Round / battle:
    ROUND_END           end of the round (used by the view)
    BATTLE_OVER         fight ended; winner determined

================================================================================
  18. AI  (AIStrategy.java, GreedyAIStrategy.java)
================================================================================

AI STRATEGY
  Interface that selects a BattlePlan for the enemy combatant each round.
  selectPlan(ai, opponent, RandomSource) receives both combatants and the shared
  RandomSource (centralized, reproducible randomness). Implementing this interface
  is the only step needed to add a new AI difficulty or behaviour.

GREEDY AI STRATEGY  (the only shipped implementation; default)
  A greedy, lightly-randomised round planner — NOT a power sort. A single loop
  keeps committing moves until none is affordable (AP and CE) or placeable.

  Selection — weighted-random pick favouring offence:
    WEIGHT_OFFENSIVE = 6.0,  WEIGHT_DEFENSIVE = 2.5,  WEIGHT_UTILITY = 1.0.
    Candidates are partitioned into offensive (ATTACK tag) / defensive
    (move.isDefensive()) / utility. Defensive moves are gated out until at least
    one offensive segment is placed (unless no offensive move is possible).
    A weighted pool is rolled each iteration. The same move may be committed
    multiple times in a round while budgets allow.

  Affordability filter — known moves that fit both AP and CE budgets
    (plan.fitsBudgets), are not locked by an ability (lockedMoveTags), and are
    not in this round's "stuck" set.

  Placement:
    Offensive / utility moves — grouped early on the offensive board, placed
      right after the last segment (tick 1 on an empty board) with a small
      random 0–2 tick gap.
    Defensive moves — their FIRE tick is aligned to a randomly chosen placed
      offensive segment's fire tick (start = max(1, targetFireTick − unleashPoint + 1)),
      so the block is active when the attack lands. This same-tick alignment only
      contests an attack when the AI is fast enough to fire first (defenses only
      protect if they've already fired); when the AI is strictly slower the
      alignment is skipped and the defense falls back to grouped-early so its
      budget isn't wasted on a tick where it can't contest. Also falls back to
      grouped-early if alignment cannot be placed.

  Termination — because the timeline only accumulates, a move that fails to
    place once can never fit later this round; it is added to a per-round "stuck"
    set and never retried.

================================================================================
  19. EDITORS  (graphics .../screens/editors/*.java)
================================================================================

All four editors are LibGDX Scene2D screens extending EditorScreenBase<D> with
shared CRUD chrome (master-detail layout, save/delete, id resequencing). They
are NOT CLI tools. Authoring mode is enabled with -Djjktbf.authoring=true.

Re-sequencing: deleting an entity resequences all ids and remaps references in
characters, abilities, coded targets, and technique trees.

MOVE EDITOR  (MoveEditorScreen)
  Creates/edits moves; saves data/moves/all_moves.json. Form sections: identity,
  tags (TagPicker — STUN/GUARD_BREAK/HEAVY tags mirror to the dedicated boolean
  flags), cost (AP/CE), tag-conditioned Attack/Defense/Utility cards, technique
  requirement (with a live "technique found" hint), stat prerequisites, and
  free-move / must-be-granted checkboxes. The Attack card has an AOE sub-section
  (shown when AOE-tagged): an AOE Type dropdown (Multiple Targets / All Enemies /
  All Others) and, for Multiple, a Target Count field. Move effects (self /
  on-hit / on-block / on-parry / on-dodge) are authored as rows via a modal that
  toggles between Status / Coded / Summon flavours — summoning a shikigami is now
  an effect row, not a MISC toggle. Validation runs MoveData.toMove() (the same
  path the engine uses) and requires exactly one of ATTACK/UTILITY/DEFENSIVE;
  an ATTACK needs a damage-type tag; a DEFENSIVE needs a block type or coded
  self-effect. Field ranges: AP cost 1..999, unleash point 1..999, power/CE
  0..99999, accuracy 0..100%, block reduction 0..100%, block duration -1..99999,
  prerequisites 0..300.

CHARACTER EDITOR  (CharacterEditorScreen)
  Creates/edits characters; saves data/characters/all_characters.json. Sections:
  identity (name/description/title/sprite/innate-technique select), a Type dropdown
  (Sorcerer / Shikigami), a directly-selectable checkbox, a Manual / Point-Buy
  toggle, ten StatField sliders, a live derived-stat preview (HP, AP bar, Acc,
  Evasion, CE pool, Combat Arts / Jujutsu Arts slot counts), a MoveAssignmentPanel
  (slot-gated, technique/prerequisite-filtered, drag-and-drop), an ability
  assignment panel, and a SkillTreeCanvas per accessible technique.

  title        Optional free-text display epithet (e.g. "SHIKIGAMI",
               "The Honored One"). Display-only — carries no combat rules. Blank
               = no title shown.
  type         CharacterType: SORCERER (default, directly selectable) or SHIKIGAMI
               (a summoned spirit — Divine Dogs, Mahoraga — not directly selectable
               unless overridden; only SHIKIGAMI definitions may be summoned by a
               move/ability summon effect).

  Constants: STAT_MIN=10, STAT_MAX=300, BASELINE=80,
    POINT_BUDGET_WITH_TECHNIQUE     = 1000
    POINT_BUDGET_WITHOUT_TECHNIQUE  = 1080   (+80 when CTM is locked at N/A)
  Point-buy math: budget = base + resolvedAbilities(cd).statBonusPoints() (a
  STAT_BONUS_POINTS ability extends the budget); pointsSpent sums (stat − 80)
  across stats, skipping CTM when the character has no technique.
  STAT_ALLOCATION_MINIMUM effects raise individual slider floors and save-time
  validation without changing battle-time stats or blocking temporary debuffs.

ABILITY EDITOR  (AbilityEditorScreen)
  Creates/edits abilities; saves data/abilities/all_abilities.json. Category
  {PASSIVE, ACTIVE}; sourceType {CHARACTER, TECHNIQUE, MOVE, STAT_THRESHOLD,
  ABILITY}. Every ability uses EffectListEditor; CODED rows select an allow-listed
  compiled feature and remain addable, editable, and removable like generic rows.
  Active abilities with generic effects use ConditionTreeEditor (nested AND/OR)
  and an optional activation chance (0–100%). Validation enforces unique name,
  valid category/source, source-specific references, effect parameters, and the
  passive/active restrictions owned by each effect type.

TECHNIQUE EDITOR  (TechniqueEditorScreen)
  Creates/edits innate techniques and their skill trees; saves
  data/techniques/all_techniques.json. A SkillTreeCanvas authoring surface:
  drag nodes, right-click to attach/edit prerequisites. Save rejects duplicate
  names, rewrites technique-name references in moves (requiredTechniqueId),
  abilities (sourceValue / UNLOCK_TECHNIQUE), and characters
  (innateTechniqueName) on rename, and writes mastery/stat prerequisites back
  onto the technique's moves and the mastery threshold onto its abilities.
  Delete is blocked while any move or ability references the technique.

  Skill tree prerequisites (SkillTreePrerequisiteData): NODE (nodeId, attached),
  MASTERY (minimum), STAT (stat, minimum).
